PS5 WebKit Autoloader

Flash a 4 MB merged image, then serve srbraboo’s autoloader from the ESP32 SoftAP.

Chrome or Edge. Hold BOOT, plug USB, then connect.

The chip is detected automatically. After flash, USB serial drops — that is expected. Unplug and move the board to the PS5.

On the PS5

  1. Join ESP32_PORTAL / 12345678.
  2. Open Settings → Guide & Tips → User’s Guide.
  3. Wait for cache and install. Leave the ESP32 plugged in.
  4. Later boots: Media → WebKit Autoloader. Stay on the SoftAP.

Relapse needs an IP on the PS5. The SoftAP provides it. No internet. Poops does not need a network interface.
